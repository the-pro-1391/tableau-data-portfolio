# 116-Year India Historical Rainfall & Climate Risk Analysis

Welcome to my Tableau analytics portfolio! This repository contains a comprehensive geospatial and time-series analysis of India's climate patterns, focusing on historical rainfall, drought risks, and seasonal flood extremes.

---

## 1. Climate Risk Map
This dashboard utilizes geospatial plotting (latitude/longitude) and time-series forecasting to track century-long rainfall trends. It includes a dynamic matrix to monitor monthly flood risk across all subdivisions.

<img src="Climate risk map image.png" alt="Climate Risk Map Dashboard" width="800">

* **Skills Showcased:** Geospatial Mapping, Time-Series Forecasting, Conditional Formatting, Matrix Development.

---

## 2. The Monsoon Blueprint
This dashboard leverages Level of Detail (LOD) expressions and cross-filtering to provide a precise regional ranking of seasonal monsoon impacts, allowing stakeholders to identify high-risk zones instantly.

<img src="Monsoon blueprint image.png" alt="Monsoon Blueprint Dashboard" width="800">

* **Skills Showcased:** Tableau Dashboard Design, LOD Expressions, Advanced Sorting, Cross-Filtering, Data Storytelling.
